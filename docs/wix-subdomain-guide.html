Keeping your Wix portal alive when the new site goes live

Your students keep logging in, paying and booking exactly where they do now. The new website just needs the front door, and Wix moves to a side entrance called portal.thanailartistry.com.

The question everyone asks

Do I have to buy anything? No.

A subdomain is a free label on a domain you already own. portal.thanailartistry.com costs $0 and takes about 20 minutes of clicking. You do not buy a new domain, a new Wix plan, or anything else.

What goes where

New website · publicwww.thanailartistry.comHome, programs, tuition, workshops, services, Spanish version. Built by Smart Scale, hosted on Vercel.
Wix · back officeportal.thanailartistry.comStudent login, payment plans, applications, tour and workshop bookings, store and forms. Nothing changes inside.

Find out who manages your domain

Gen · 2 minutes

Log in to Wix and open Settings then Domains. Look at thanailartistry.com.

  • If it says the domain was purchased through Wix, you will do every DNS step inside Wix.
  • If it was connected from GoDaddy, Namecheap, Google or another registrar, DNS lives there. Same steps, different login.

Menu wording in Wix changes from time to time. If a label looks slightly different, look for the same idea.

Create the subdomain and point it at your Wix site

Gen · 5 minutes

Domain bought through Wix:

  1. In Settings › Domains, open the menu next to thanailartistry.com and choose the option to add or manage subdomains.
  2. Type portal and assign it to this Wix site.

Domain managed at a registrar:

  1. In Wix, choose Connect a domain you already own and enter portal.thanailartistry.com.
  2. Wix shows you a CNAME value. Add this record at your registrar:
TypeHost / NameValue
CNAMEportalthe value Wix shows you
Wait: a few minutes to 24 hours for Wix to show it as connected

Make the portal address the primary one in Wix

Gen · 1 minute

Once it shows as connected, set portal.thanailartistry.com as the primary domain for the Wix site. Keep www attached for now. We only remove it after the new site is live, so students never see a gap.

Point the main domain at the new website

Gen with Smart Scale on the phone · 5 minutes

In the same DNS manager from step 1, add or edit these two records. Smart Scale will confirm both values on launch day.

TypeHost / NameValue
A@76.76.21.21
CNAMEwwwcname.vercel-dns.com

If Wix manages your DNS and will not let www point away from Wix, the cleanest fix is to move DNS management to the registrar or to Vercel. Smart Scale will walk you through that call.

Remove www from the Wix site

Gen · 1 minute · after the new site loads

Open www.thanailartistry.com in a private window. When the new site appears, go back to Settings › Domains and disconnect www.thanailartistry.com from the Wix site. Wix keeps working at the portal address.

Smart Scale flips one switch

Smart Scale · 1 minute

Every link on the new site that goes into Wix (Apply, Student Login, Book a Tour, workshops, services, the store) is built from a single setting. We change it to https://portal.thanailartistry.com, redeploy, and every link updates at once.

Good to know

Will students have to make new accounts?

No. Accounts, payment plans, bookings and history all live in Wix and stay exactly as they are. Only the web address of the portal changes.

What if a student has the old www login link bookmarked?

It lands on the new website, where the Student Login button in the header and footer takes them straight to the portal. Smart Scale can also add a redirect for the old account page.

Does my Wix plan change?

No. You keep the same plan, the same apps and the same billing. The only Wix setting that changes is which address the site answers to.

Can I keep editing Wix pages?

Yes. Bookings, store items, forms and prices still get edited in Wix. The new site links to those pages by their address, so keep the page URLs the same when you edit them.

What is DNS, in plain words?

DNS is the address book for your domain. Each record says "this name goes to this place." We are adding one line for the portal and changing two lines for www and the root domain. Nothing else is touched.
